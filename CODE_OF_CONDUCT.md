# Code of Conduct

English | [繁體中文](docs/zh-TW/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Commitment

TripReplay is a community project open to everyone. Participants are expected to make collaboration respectful, constructive and free from harassment, regardless of background, identity, experience level or language.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communication.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Accept contributions and questions in either English or Traditional Chinese.
- Respect others' privacy, including the personal travel information that may appear in bug reports.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information, such as locations, photos or contact details, without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel operated by the project — and when an individual represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact methods listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters will not face retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report and may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed from their role.
