# TripReplay

English | [繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md)

TripReplay turns your itinerary, photos and videos into an animated map recap of your trip. Everything is processed on your device.

> **Status:** Design phase. There is no usable build yet.

## Planned Features

- Build an itinerary with stops grouped by day, across multiple countries and time zones.
- Import photos and videos; stops are suggested automatically from GPS data and capture time.
- Generate an animated top-down map recap: the route draws between stops, the camera zooms into each location, and your media plays in order.
- Add your own background music and choose a theme.
- Preview the recap on a scrubbable timeline in 16:9 or 9:16.
- Play the recap full-screen in the browser.
- Download the map of the regions you need once; after that, everything works without a connection, including place search.
- After 1.0: export to MP4 (1080p or 720p, 30 fps).
- Version 1.0 runs in the browser and can be installed as a Progressive Web App, and comes as an Android application (APK); installed apps for Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS will follow.

## Privacy

Your photos, videos, metadata and projects never leave your device. There is no account, no analytics and no cloud processing. The network is used only to load the application and to download the maps you choose; place search runs on your device, and there is no TripReplay server. See the [Privacy Statement](docs/privacy.md) for every network request the application makes.
